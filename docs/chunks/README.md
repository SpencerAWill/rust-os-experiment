# Chunks

Status: Living (2026-11-17). One folder per chunk of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): its spec, plan and log. The roadmap is the index of chunks, with each one's status and links.

## When this applies

- You start, resume or review a chunk ([workflow](../workflow.md)).
- You want to know how something was designed, or why a spec says what it says.

How far to trust a spec, a plan and a log: the `chunks/` row of [docs/README.md](../README.md#contents); how chunk folders are named and when a plan becomes historical: its [conventions](../README.md#conventions).

## Contents

- `_template/`: `spec.md`, `plan.md` and `log.md`, copied into a new chunk's folder.
- `m*/`: one folder per chunk, `m<N>.<n>-<slug>/`, holding `spec.md`, `plan.md` and `log.md` .
